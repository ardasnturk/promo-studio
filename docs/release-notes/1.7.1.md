## Promo Studio 1.7.1

### YouTube from your iPhone

With Remote Code on your iPhone you can now share to YouTube as you do on the
Mac:

* Choose a post's YouTube channel, or keep it off YouTube or off Instagram.
* Edit its YouTube title, or have Claude write one.
* Post to YouTube only, or to Instagram and YouTube together.
* See whether the video is live, waiting for its time or kept private by
  YouTube, with its views, likes and comments.

This needs Remote Code for iOS 1.8.1 or later.

Includes everything from [1.7.0](https://github.com/ardasnturk/promo-studio/releases/tag/v1.7.0).
