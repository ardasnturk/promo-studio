## Promo Studio 1.5.0

### Stories, photo posts and carousels

Promo Studio now posts every Instagram format, not just Reels.

* **Share as** in the publish panel: a video goes out as a **Reel** or a
  **story**; an image post as a **photo**, a **carousel** or **stories**.
  Scheduled posts keep the choice. A 9:16 image can only be a story, since
  Instagram's feed allows 4:5 at most.
* **Carousels**: set the number of slides (2–10) in New Video → Image. Claude
  designs one scene per slide in one visual system (a hook, one idea per
  slide, a closing call to action), and each slide is rendered as its own
  JPEG. The preview pages through them, and **Save Slides…** saves them all.
  Posted as stories, each slide becomes one story.
* Story insights (views, reach, shares) are read while they are available.

### How pictures reach Instagram

Instagram's API takes pictures only from a public web address. While a
picture is published, Promo Studio serves just those files through a
temporary [Cloudflare quick tunnel](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-one/connections/connect-networks/do-more-with-tunnels/trycloudflare/)
(`cloudflared`, no Cloudflare account) at a random trycloudflare.com address,
and closes it as soon as Instagram has them. Nothing is uploaded anywhere
else. Videos are uploaded directly as before and don't need it.

* The setup checklist has a **cloudflared** row with an **Install** button
  (Homebrew). The Homebrew cask now installs it with the app.

### Remote Code

With the next Remote Code iOS build, choose Reel, Post, Carousel or Story from
the phone, swipe through a carousel and save or share all its slides. In the
control API, posts have `placement`, `canPostToFeed` and `media.slides`,
`settings.canPostPictures` is new, `publish` and `schedule` take `placement`,
`setPlacement` is new and `createDirectedPost` takes `slides`
(docs/CONTROL_API.md).

Includes everything from [1.4.0](https://github.com/ardasnturk/promo-studio/releases/tag/v1.4.0).
