## Promo Studio 1.4.0

### Errors you can act on

When something fails, Promo Studio now says what happened in plain words and
what fixes it, with the fix as a button. The raw output is still there under
**Details**.

* Recognized: Claude Code not installed or signed out, Claude's usage limit
  (with the time it resets), Claude running out of steps or stopping without
  a result, the voiceover quota, a render error in the video's code, a render
  timeout, an expired Instagram session, Instagram's limits, a rejected video,
  no internet, a full disk.
* **Try Again** runs only the step that failed; the storyboard, the code and
  the video that already exist are kept.
* **Let Claude Fix It**: when the render stops on an error in the video's
  code, Claude reads the error, fixes the code and renders again.
* **Produce Without Voiceover** and **Reconnect Account** where they help.
* The setup checklist also checks disk space, Instagram sessions that have
  expired or are about to, and Codex.

### Real people, real photos

Videos no longer draw players as silhouettes or stick figures. Claude lists
the real people a video shows; Promo Studio downloads a photo of each
(Wikipedia, then Wikidata, then a link Claude found) and cuts them out of the
background on the Mac with Vision.

* New kit components: **Portrait** (moods: fierce, heroic, mono, natural; a
  rim light in the team color) and **FaceOff** (two people face to face with a
  VS).
* **Edit photos with Codex** (Settings → Photos): when a video needs a look a
  photo doesn't have, such as a fierce expression, looking at the camera or a
  celebration, Claude asks Codex's image generation to edit it. It runs on
  your ChatGPT plan with no extra cost. The storyboard's **People** box shows
  the original and the edit side by side: pick one, or give your own
  instruction and edit again.
* Posts with edited photos remind you to label them as AI-made on Instagram
  and can add a note to the caption.

### Your images, and images instead of videos

* **Your Images** in New Video: add photos and Claude looks at them and builds
  the video around them; people and objects in them are cut out too.
* **Image posts**: New Video can make a single picture (portrait 4:5, square
  or story). Claude writes a design brief, and the image is made after you
  approve it. Save or share it and post it from Instagram: Instagram's API can
  publish pictures only from a public web address, which Promo Studio doesn't
  have.

### Remote Code

Everything above works from the phone with Remote Code 1.5.1: the fix
buttons, Mac Status, photos from the photo library, image posts with Save to
Photos, and choosing or re-editing people's photos. In the control API, posts
and alerts carry a `problem`, the state has `health`, and `uploadImage`,
`fix`, `checkHealth`, `setPersonPhoto` and `editPersonPhoto` are new
(docs/CONTROL_API.md).

Includes everything from [1.3.1](https://github.com/ardasnturk/promo-studio/releases/tag/v1.3.1).
