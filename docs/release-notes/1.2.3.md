## Promo Studio 1.2.3

### Fixed

* **Voiceover no longer drops the first sound of a line.** When the voiceover
  was split into scenes, each line was cut too close to its first loud syllable,
  so quiet opening consonants were lost ("Fenerbahçe" played as "nerbahçe",
  "Kickoff" as "ckoff"). Lines now keep a short lead-in and a fading tail,
  without reaching into the neighbouring lines.
* Videos voiced with an earlier version are voiced again the next time you
  produce them, so they get the fix too.

Includes everything from [1.2.2](https://github.com/ardasnturk/promo-studio/releases/tag/v1.2.2).
