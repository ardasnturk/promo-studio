## Promo Studio 1.6.1

### Fixed

Instagram sometimes refuses a video upload with a generic "Request processing
failed", even when the video is fine. Promo Studio now handles that by itself.

* **A dropped upload** goes on from where it stopped, up to three times.
* **A refused upload** starts again on a new Instagram upload, so you don't have
  to press **Try Again**.
* **If it still fails**, the error says how much Instagram received and the
  reason it gives, when it gives one.

This works for Reels and video stories.
