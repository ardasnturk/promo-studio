## Promo Studio 1.2.2

### Added

* **Choose the cover.** The Publish tab has a new Cover section. "Choose
  Cover…" lets you scrub through the video and pick any frame as the cover.
* **Real logos.** When a video shows real clubs, leagues or brands, Claude lists
  their official logo images (from the app's own data source or Wikimedia
  Commons). Promo Studio downloads them and the video shows them, instead of
  logos drawn in code. A logo that can't be found falls back to a colour badge.

### Fixed

* **The cover reaches Instagram.** The Reel was posted without a cover frame,
  so Instagram used the first frame, which is often blank or mid-animation. The
  chosen cover is now sent with the post.
* The automatic cover is taken from the scene Claude picks as the most
  eye-catching, not always from 1.2 seconds in.
* The indeterminate progress bar now moves across the whole bar instead of a
  narrow strip.

### Remote Code

* New `setCover` action (`postId`, `sec`); posts carry `coverSec`.

Includes everything from [1.2.1](https://github.com/ardasnturk/promo-studio/releases/tag/v1.2.1).
