## Promo Studio 1.2.0

### Added

* **Free-form videos.** A third video type next to the promo film and the
  quick template: *Free-form video (directed by Claude)*. Describe any video,
  such as a match report in the style of a news bulletin, a story or an
  explainer, and Claude makes exactly that, with a storyboard you approve
  first. The app's features, screenshots or a download call appear only where
  your request asks for them, for example only in the closing scene.

### Improved

* **Foreign names in the voiceover.** The voiceover is read with the video
  language's pronunciation, so foreign names (players, clubs, brands) used to
  come out mangled. Claude now writes them in the voiceover the way they are
  pronounced, while the on-screen text keeps their original spelling. Scores
  and times are written as they are spoken.

### Fixed

* The updater quits Promo Studio from the run loop instead of from the update
  job, so installing an update can't hang the app.

Includes everything from [1.1.1](https://github.com/ardasnturk/promo-studio/releases/tag/v1.1.1).
