## Promo Studio 1.1.0

### Know when it's done

* **Remaining time** — running jobs show how long they still need ("About
  3 min left") in the studio, in the menu bar panel and on your iPhone in
  Remote Code. Renders are estimated from their progress; Claude's steps
  (storyboard, coding the video) from how long the last ones took on your Mac,
  after the first couple of videos.
* **Notifications with Remote Code** — with [Remote Code](https://github.com/ardasnturk/remote-code)
  1.4 on the same Mac, you get a notification on the Mac and on your phone when
  a video is ready, a storyboard waits for your approval, a post went out or a
  job failed — also while the phone app is closed. Tapping it opens the video.
