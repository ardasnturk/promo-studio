How it works

From your code to a finished post in four steps.

  1. 1

    It reads your project

    Point it at your app's project folder, whatever it's written in. Claude Code finds the app's features, brand colors and screenshots, and can capture new screens in the iOS Simulator.

  2. 2

    You describe the video

    Write what you want, or add your own images. Claude researches current facts on the web when needed and writes a scene-by-scene storyboard with its sources.

  3. 3

    Claude codes it

    After you approve the storyboard, Claude writes a custom Remotion composition, renders stills to check its own work and fixes what doesn't look right. Revise it in plain words.

  4. 4

    Render and post

    The Mac renders the video or image. Post it now, schedule it, or save it, then follow views and reach in the app.

Features

A whole studio in your menu bar.

Directed films

Every video is its own Remotion code: counters, phone shots, matchups, charts and transitions, from a kit made for short vertical video.

Real photos and logos

It finds real club crests and photos of real people on the web and cuts them out. With Codex on your ChatGPT plan it can change an expression or pose, at no extra cost.

Your own images

Add photos from your Mac or phone. Claude looks at them, builds the post around them and can place the cut-out subject over a new background.

Research with sources

Dates, line-ups and prices are checked with web search before they go on screen, and the sources stay with the post.

Voiceover and music

Optional Gemini text-to-speech fitted to each scene, a royalty-free beat and sound effects timed to the motion.

Ten languages

Videos, captions and the app itself in English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Português, 日本語, 한국어 and 简体中文.

Planned posts

Write a request now and have it made tomorrow at 15:00. Approve and post it yourself, or let it run from start to finish.

Errors you can act on

When a step fails, it says what happened in plain words and offers the fix as a button: try again, let Claude repair the code, reconnect an account.

From your iPhone

With Remote Code, create, approve and post from your phone while the Mac does the work.

Promo Templates

Yours

Your code, keys and videos stay on your Mac.

  • Your own Claude Code. Promo Studio drives the claude CLI signed in with your account; there is no Promo Studio server or account.
  • Secrets in the Keychain. Instagram tokens, the Meta app secret and the Gemini key live in your login keychain.
  • No telemetry. Nothing is collected about you or what you make. The code is MIT-licensed on GitHub.

FAQ

Questions

What does it cost?

Promo Studio is free. It uses the Claude Code plan you already have. Voiceover uses your own Gemini API key, and photo edits use Codex on your ChatGPT plan; both are optional.

Which Instagram accounts can it post to?

Business and Creator accounts, through Instagram's official API, with Instagram Login (no Facebook Page needed) or Facebook Login. Meta may limit stories to Business accounts.

Do I need a Mac app project?

No. Projects make promo films about an app, but you can also make standalone videos and images from a prompt: news, stories, explainers, match previews.

Why does it need cloudflared?

Videos are uploaded directly, but Instagram downloads pictures from a web address. cloudflared opens a temporary, random trycloudflare.com link for just those files while they are published. It needs no Cloudflare account and nothing is stored online.

Can I use the videos elsewhere?

Yes. Save any video as an MP4 or any picture as a JPEG and post it to TikTok, YouTube Shorts or anywhere else.
