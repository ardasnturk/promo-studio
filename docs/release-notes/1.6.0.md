## Promo Studio 1.6.0

### Planned posts

Scheduling used to mean posting a finished video later. Now the making itself
can wait: write a request today and have it made tomorrow at 15:00, with
research as of that moment.

* **Make it later** in New Video: pick a time. Nothing is made and no Claude
  usage is spent until then. It works for videos, images, carousels and
  templates, and your images are kept with the request.
* **Approve the storyboard automatically** and **Post automatically when
  it's ready** decide how far it goes on its own. Both are off by default: it
  stops at the storyboard and waits for you, as any post does. With both on,
  it runs from start to finish, and you choose Reel, Post, Carousel or Story
  up front.
* Until its time, a planned post can be edited, started with **Start Now** or
  deleted. Planned posts have their own group in the calendar and appear under
  Up next in the menu bar.
* If a step fails, the plan stops and shows the fix; **Try Again** carries on
  from there, up to posting.

Promo Studio has to be open and the Mac awake at that time; otherwise the plan
starts when they are. A plan that starts over an hour late is made but not
posted automatically, so a post about tonight doesn't go out tomorrow. The Mac
stays awake while a plan runs.

### Fixed

* **Try Again** was missing when publishing an image post failed.

### Remote Code

With the next Remote Code iOS build, plan posts from the phone and change them
until their time. In the control API, posts have `plan`,
`settings.canPlan` is new, `updatePlan` and `startPlan` are new, and
`createDirectedPost` and `generateFromSkill` take `plan` and `placement`
(docs/CONTROL_API.md).

Includes everything from [1.5.0](https://github.com/ardasnturk/promo-studio/releases/tag/v1.5.0).
