## Promo Studio 1.7.0

### YouTube Shorts

Your videos can now go to YouTube as Shorts, as well as or instead of Instagram.

* **Connect a channel** in Settings → YouTube. You create an OAuth client
  ("Desktop app") in your own Google Cloud project, at no cost, and sign in
  through your browser. The steps are in
  [docs/SETUP.md](https://github.com/ardasnturk/promo-studio/blob/main/docs/SETUP.md#youtube).
* **Choose where each post goes** on its Publish tab: Instagram, YouTube or
  both. The first channel you connect is the default for new videos.
* **A title for YouTube:** Claude writes one when YouTube is chosen. The
  caption and hashtags become the description, with `#Shorts`.
* **Scheduled Shorts go out on time even if the Mac is off.** Scheduling
  uploads the video right away and YouTube publishes it at its time.
* **Uploads resume** after a dropped connection, and when one place fails,
  trying again only redoes that one.
* **Views, likes and comments** from YouTube appear on the post.
* Videos showing photos edited with AI are marked as altered content on
  YouTube.

**Important:** until Google audits your Cloud project, YouTube keeps every
video uploaded through it private, and those videos can't be made public
later. Promo Studio tells you when this happens. Apply for the free audit with
the [YouTube API Services – Audit and Quota Extension form](https://support.google.com/youtube/contact/yt_api_form).

Includes everything from [1.6.3](https://github.com/ardasnturk/promo-studio/releases/tag/v1.6.3).
